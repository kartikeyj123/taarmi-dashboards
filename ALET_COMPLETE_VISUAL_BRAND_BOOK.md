## 1. BRAND PHILOSOPHY & THE SCIENCE OF DOPAMINE

### 1.1 The Brand Origin
**ALET** (/uh-LET/) was born from a fundamental frustration in modern fashion:  
*Traditional luxury handbags are gorgeous on the outside, but dark, disorganised chaos on the inside. Functional work bags, on the other hand, are clinical, bulky, and devoid of joy.*

ALET bridges this divide. We engineer women's contemporary everyday bags, shoulder bags, and totes that harmonize **sculptural exterior aesthetics** with **micro-engineered internal organization**.

### 1.2 The Neurochemistry of "Dopamine Dressing"
The visual identity of ALET integrates the literal molecular formula of **Dopamine** ($C_8H_{11}NO_2$) — the biological neurotransmitter governing:
- **Anticipation & Motivation:** The thrill of walking out the door knowing your entire day is organized.
- **Reward & Accomplishment:** Opening your bag and instantly retrieving your keys, cards, or lip balm in under 2 seconds.
- **Sensory Delight:** The tactile satisfaction of premium pebble-grain vegan leather, heavy cold-touch metal hardware, and water-repellent jacquard lining.

### 1.3 The Brand Manifesto
> *"We do not sell bags. We sell momentum.  
> We believe that when your essentials are effortlessly contained, your mind is free to conquer the room.  
> Every zipper glide, every gold-rimmed stud, and every intuitive pocket is calibrated to deliver a quiet rush of confidence.  
> ALET is your chemistry, elevated."*

### 1.4 The Tagline Hierarchy
- **Master Global Tagline:** **YOUR DAILY DOSE OF DELIGHT.**
- **Operational Brand Signature:** **Everyday, Elevated.**
- **Campaign & Editorial Lines:**
  * *Carry Your Chemistry.*
  * *Engineered for Joy. Crafted for Motion.*
  * *Zero Chaos. Pure Grace.*

### 1.5 Brand Positioning Matrix
- **Category:** Accessible Everyday Lifestyle (Women's Handbags, Totes, Slings).
- **Core Competitive Space:**
  * *Vs. Zouk:* Shared love for vibrant, cruelty-free, modern Indian lifestyles; elevated by sleeker minimalist modern silhouettes and higher-end metal hardware.
  * *Vs. Mokobara:* Shared passion for hyper-organized utility, airport luggage pass-throughs, and durable materials; infused with feminine chic and editorial fashion confidence.

## 2. THE LOGO SYSTEM & CONSTRUCTION STANDARDS

### 2.1 Logo Symbol Anatomy
The official ALET mark consists of two inseparable elements:
1. **The Dopamine Molecular Skeleton (Left/Top):**
   - The central 6-carbon benzene ring featuring interior double-bond strokes.
   - Two oxygen-hydroxyl terminal nodes on the left stems (C3 and C4 positions).
   - The ethylamine aliphatic side chain rising dynamically upward-right at a 30° angle, dropping to a valley, and culminating in the circular amino node.
2. **The Alet Wordmark (Right/Bottom):**
   - Title-case typography: Capital **A**, lowercase **l**, **e**, **t**.
   - Custom geometric sans-serif with a bold stem weight, generous x-height, and a confident forward posture tucked securely under the ethylamine arm.

### 2.2 Master Logo Configurations
1. **The Square Master Mark (`Alet_Square.png`):**
   - Aspect ratio: 1:1.
   - Purpose: Social avatars, dust bag centers, swing tag seals, circular metal medallions, box lids.
2. **The Horizontal Master Mark (`Alet_Rectangle.png`):**
   - Aspect ratio: ~2.1:1.
   - Purpose: Amazon Storefront banners, website desktop header, horizontal bag hardware plaques, shipping carton side-panels.

### 2.3 Clear Space & Protection Zone
- **Rule of Isolation:** No graphic element, text, stitch line, or margin may enter the clear space zone.
- **Calculation:** The clear space around all edges is equal to **1.5× the height of the lowercase 'e'** in the Alet wordmark.
- **Minimum Digital Resolution:** 
  * Horizontal: 120 px width minimum.
  * Square: 48 × 48 px minimum.
- **Minimum Physical Print / Stamping Size:**
  * Laser-engraved metal hardware: 28 mm width minimum.
  * Screen-print on textile: 40 mm width minimum.

### 2.4 Unacceptable Logo Treatments (Strict Prohibitions)
1. **Never alter the chemical structure:** Do not remove the hydroxyl nodes or modify the side-chain angles.
2. **Never apply generic drop shadows or bevel filters:** Depth must be achieved through authentic physical material finishes (stamping, debossing, PVD metal coating).
3. **Never stretch, condense, or skew the mark:** Proportions must remain strictly 1:1 locked.
4. **Never alter the font case:** "ALET" all-caps is permitted solely as plain text in running copy; the visual logo mark must always remain title-case "Alet".
5. **Never place over visually noisy patterns without a solid contrast field:** Minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 (WCAG AA) must be preserved.

### 3.2 Material Science Specifications
1. **Exterior Vegan Leather (Microfiber-PU Composite):**
   - Texture: Fine pebble grain with a soft, buttery hand-feel.
   - Thickness: 1.2 mm ± 0.1 mm with high-density woven microfiber backing.
   - Testing Standards: Hydrolysis resistance > 5 years (Jungle Test 10 weeks); Martindale Abrasion Test > 50,000 cycles; Water-repellent topcoat.
2. **Interior Jacquard Fabric:**
   - 210D High-density recycled polyester jacquard weave.
   - Double-side PU coating for spill and stain resistance (pen ink and coffee easily wipeable with a damp cloth).
3. **Hardware Alloy:**
   - Virgin Zinc Alloy (Zamak 3 / Zamak 5).
   - Electroplating: 3-layer PVD finish (Copper base → Nickel barrier → Champagne Gold topcoat) with clear electrophoretic baking lacquer.
   - Salt Spray Test: 72 hours grade 10 (zero oxidation, zero green patina).

## 4. TYPOGRAPHIC HIERARCHY & EDITORIAL SCALE

### 4.1 Typeface Selection Rationale
- **Display Hero:** **Syne** (Weights: SemiBold 600, ExtraBold 800)  
  *Why:* Architectural, sculpted curves that evoke contemporary European couture and fearless design authority.
- **Section Heading & Structural UI:** **Outfit** (Weights: Medium 500, SemiBold 600, Bold 700)  
  *Why:* Open geometric counters, ultra-clean cadence, and friendly legibility.
- **Functional Body, Micro-specs & Care Labels:** **Plus Jakarta Sans** (Weights: Regular 400, Medium 500)  
  *Why:* Built with humanistic optical corrections for exceptional mobile reading on small screens and care tags.
- **Technical Codes & Dimensions:** **JetBrains Mono**  
  *Why:* Crisp tabular numbers for specs, serial numbers, and barcodes.

## 5. SIGNATURE MONOGRAM & JACQUARD SURFACE PATTERN

### 5.1 The "Molecular Grid" Surface Pattern
To establish instant interior luxury and unboxing delight, ALET utilizes a proprietary repeating jacquard pattern:
- **Geometry:** Repeating 60° diagonal isometric network of interlocking dopamine hex-rings interconnected by slim chemical bonds.
- **Scale:** Each hexagonal ring measures exactly **16 mm across flats** in the final woven fabric.
- **Weave Contrast:** Subtle tone-on-tone semi-matte Champagne thread woven across an Espresso base thread.
- **Application:**
  * 100% of internal bag lining.
  * Interior wrapping tissue paper (printed in Warm Oat Cream with subtle Terracotta ink).
  * Packaging ribbon and tape.

## 8. PHOTOGRAPHY ART DIRECTION & ANTI-SLOP VISUAL QA

### 8.1 Editorial & Lifestyle Photography (On-Model)
- **Lighting:** Natural directional daylight (morning 9:00 AM or golden hour 4:30 PM). Soft natural highlights, zero harsh artificial studio flash.
- **Setting:** Contemporary metropolitan architectural settings — clean stone steps, modern glass façades, warm café terraces, sleek metro concourses.
- **Model Posture:** Purposeful, dynamic motion (walking briskly, hailing a cab, glancing with confidence). Never passive, slumped, or staring blankly.
- **Wardrobe Harmony:** Neutral modern tailoring (oversized trench coats, tailored linen blazers, crisp white poplin shirts) that allows the Sunset Terracotta, Matcha, or Onyx bag to be the hero focus.

### 8.2 Product Still Life & Macro Detail Photography
- **Surfaces:** Textured limestone, warm Travertine marble slabs, matte linen, and architectural shadow blocks.
- **Composition:** Asymmetrical, balanced, editorial restraint.
- **Macro Shots Required for Every SKU:**
  1. *Hardware Close-Up:* Macro shot of the Champagne Gold badge and YKK zipper puller catching soft rim-light.
  2. *Interior Exploded View:* Top-down shot with the bag open, showing the contrasting blush lining, laptop snugly in sleeve, keys clipped to leash, and bottle in holster.
  3. *Stitching Precision:* Extreme close-up showing 8 stitches-per-inch clean saddle-stitching.

### 8.3 Anti-AI-Slop Visual QA Checklist (Strict Rules)
- ❌ **No Over-Smoothed AI Skin:** All model imagery must have authentic human skin texture, pores, and natural hair flyaways.
- ❌ **No Impossibly Floating Bags:** Bags must rest with realistic physical gravity, natural leather creasing, and authentic weight.
- ❌ **No Distorted Hardware Text:** The word "Alet" and the dopamine molecule nodes must be razor-sharp and technically accurate in every shot.

Strategic Thought Process & Keyword Logic
Why each keyword, match type, and modifier was chosen across the 4 campaigns.
1. Exact Match Core: Why These 15 Keywords?

The Human vs. Computer Filter: Many PPC tools make the mistake of picking targets purely because of a low ACoS figure. But if a keyword has only 1 order (like net basket for gift packing at ₹1,710 sales), that is a statistical fluke, not an evergreen asset.

Every keyword chosen for this Exact campaign meets two strict business criteria:
• Multi-Order Proof: Proven repeat demand (e.g. fruit basket for gift with 57 orders, fruit basket for wedding with 14 orders, decorative basket for gift packing with 13 orders).
• Impression Share & Longevity: High search frequency (tens of thousands of impressions), ensuring that when we bid aggressively on Exact, daily conversions continue consistently.

2. Phrase Match Harvester: Why Word-Order Locking?

Phrase match was chosen for root sequences where the order of words indicates high commercial intent. For example, a customer searching "fruit basket for wedding" has an entirely different buying intent than someone searching "wedding gift".

Funnel Isolation Mechanism: To prevent this Phrase campaign from competing against your Exact campaign, all 15 Exact keywords are added as Negative Exact here. This forces Amazon to route exact high-volume queries to Campaign 1, while Phrase only harvests long-tail extensions (e.g., "buy fruit basket for wedding online").

3. Broad Match: Deconstructing Your Zero-Repetition Compound String Architecture

Your broad match philosophy is mathematically brilliant:
• Instead of bidding on broad single words (like "basket" or "hamper"), you attach keywords into compound strings (e.g., fruit basket with net cover, wedding return gift basket).
• Across all your strings combined, there is zero repetition of the modifier tokens (wedding, return gift, net cover, dry fruit, engagement, diwali/navratri, decorative, box, room, fancy, tokri).

Why 4 standalone roots were kept PAUSED: Standalone roots like gift basket, fruit basket, wedding basket, return gift basket were deliberately paused because they would trigger on generic 2-word queries and cannibalize your specialized umbrella strings. By keeping them paused/negative, Amazon routes traffic exclusively to your high-margin compound umbrellas.

4. Festive & Shagun Campaign: Why Cultural Timing Matters?

From mid-September to November in India, over 70% of gift basket purchases are tied to specific cultural milestones: Navratri, Karwa Chauth Sargi, Diwali, Bhai Dooj, Roka, and Wedding Shagun.

Pre-Filled Food Bleed Protection: Customers searching for festive hampers frequently type keywords expecting pre-filled sweets/dry fruits (mithai, kaju katli, chocolates, haldiram). Since Taarmi sells the presentation basket/hamper, adding food terms as Negative Phrase protects your budget from immediate bounces.

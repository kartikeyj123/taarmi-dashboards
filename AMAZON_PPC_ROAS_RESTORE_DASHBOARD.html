The Market Flooding Theory

Deploying multiple product listings with non-overlapping niche keywords

🌊 Search Real Estate Domination: By creating multiple distinct listings for the same product line (e.g. 2-pack, 4-pack, 6-pack, square, rectangle), Taarmi captures multiple sponsored and organic spots on the search results page.

🎯 Zero Internal Cannibalization: Niche keywords are strictly partitioned across listings. Listing A targets basket for wedding; Listing B targets gift packing basket. Only universal superstars (like fruit basket for gift) are shared.

Amazon Solicitations API v1 Compliance Guardrails

1. Why Strict Day 5 Post-Delivery?

Days 1–4 give customers time to receive, unbox, and experience the product. Requesting too early causes friction, while waiting beyond Day 7 leads to memory decay. Day 5 represents the calibrated sweet spot capturing peak customer delight with maximum review conversion.

2. Replacement & Return Filtering

Sending a review request to a customer who initiated a return or replacement triggers immediate 1-star reviews. The engine strictly excludes any order flagged with return/replacement risks.

3. 100% Policy-Safe Format

Amazon prohibits asking for positive reviews or offering incentives. This engine uses Amazon's official standard email template containing neutral Product and Seller star ratings.
